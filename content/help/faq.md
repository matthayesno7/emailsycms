# Frequently asked questions

## The basics
**What is Mise?**
A brand asset library built for AI: your logos, photos, product shots, videos and designs in one place, organised automatically, with on-brand designs, photos and video made from them, and a connector for Claude. Your team approves everything AI makes. See [What Mise is](what-is-mise).

**Do I need Figma?**
No. Mise makes designs, photos and video itself. If you use Figma, Mise works with it. See [Figma](figma).

**Do I need Claude?**
No. Everything works in the Mise app. Claude is optional, for people who want to run Mise from a chat or build in Figma.

**Is there a free trial?**
No. Free is the way to try Mise: your brand kit, 50 files and one Create run, with no card and unlimited people. Upgrade when you need more.

**How much does it cost?**
Free costs nothing. Pro is $3,750 a year per brand, billed yearly (plus tax), with unlimited files and users and no seat fees. Enterprise starts at $15,000 a year for all your brands on one contract. See [Plans](plans).

**Do I pay per person?**
No. Mise is priced per brand. Invite everyone.

## Files
**What files can I upload?**
Images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, SVG) up to 50 MB and videos (MP4, WebM, MOV) up to 1 GB. See [Adding files](adding-files).

**Will Mise change my originals?**
Never. Edits are saved as new versions or copies, and the original is always kept. See [Versions](versions).

**Can I move my files from Google Drive or Dropbox?**
Yes: Add → Import from Drive, Dropbox or Box, or download a folder and drop it onto Assets. See [Importing](importing-from-cloud).

**How do I find a file?**
Describe it in the search box: "red product shots, landscape". See [Finding files](search).

**What happens when a photo's licence runs out?**
Set **Licence ends** on the photo (Pro). On that date it's blocked from downloads, sharing and new work automatically. See [No longer available](availability).

**Can I get my files out?**
Yes. Download any file in its original format from its page. For a full export of a large library, send a request through Feedback.

## Creating
**Who owns what Mise makes?**
You do. It's saved to your library.

**Why is my new photo or video in Review?**
Everything AI makes from scratch lands as a draft so a person can check it before it's used. Approve it in Review.

**Why does a design use the wrong photo?**
Create only uses approved, available photos. Approve the photos you want used, archive ones you don't, and add imagery rules to your brand kit.

**Which AI models does Mise use?**
Claude for designs, organising and search; Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana Pro and Imagen for photos; Veo for video. Mise picks the right one for each job and shows which it used. See [Create photos and video](create-photos-video).

## Sharing
**Can people download without an account?**
Yes, from share links and public portals, in the formats you allow. Invite-only portals ask them to confirm their email.

**Can I see who downloaded what?**
Share links and portals show views and downloads. Invite-only portals show who downloaded. Your own team isn't counted.

**What happens to my links if I stop paying?**
They stop opening for people outside your team until you upgrade again. Your files stay.

## Account and team
**How do I add my team?**
Settings → Team → Invite. See [Team and roles](team-and-roles).

**Can I limit what people can do?**
On Enterprise: admin, editor, contributor and viewer roles. On Free and Pro, everyone you invite is an editor.

**How do I delete my brand?**
Settings → Brand workspace → Delete this brand (owners). See [Brand settings](settings).

**How do I delete my account?**
Leave or delete your brands, then send a request through Feedback to delete your account and personal data.
