# Activity log

**Settings → Activity** shows who added, approved, changed, deleted and shared what in your brand, plus changes to people, the brand kit, product feeds and the plan. It's for **admins and owners on Enterprise**.

Entries can't be edited or removed by anyone. Deleting the brand deletes its activity log too, so download a CSV first if you need to keep it.

## What's recorded
| Group | Events |
|---|---|
| **Files** | Added, approved, moved back to review, renamed, edited (a new version), availability changed (archived, expired, obsolete, made available), deleted. |
| **People** | Invited, joined, removed, role changed. |
| **Sharing** | Share links created, turned off or deleted; portals created, published, unpublished, access changed or deleted. |
| **Brand** | Brand kit saved and approved; product feeds synced (with how many products were added or removed); plan changes. |

Each entry shows when, who, what, and the details (for example "Editor → Viewer").

Changes made by Claude through your connector, by product feed syncs, or by licence dates expiring show as **Mise (Claude connector or automation)** when there's no person to name. Files Claude adds show the person whose connector added them.

## Filter and export
- **All**, **Files**, **People**, **Sharing**, **Brand** to narrow it down.
- **Everyone** dropdown to see one person's activity.
- **Download CSV** downloads what's shown, for audits or your own records.
- **Show older** loads earlier entries.

## Good to know
Mise records activity for every brand, on every plan. When a brand moves to Enterprise, its history is already there.
