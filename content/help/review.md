# The Review page

**Review** is where Mise puts anything it shouldn't decide on its own. The number in the sidebar shows how many things are waiting. When there's nothing, you'll see "Nothing needs you".

At the top is a weekly summary: new files, how many were organised, how many were made with AI, and duplicates caught.

## What can appear

| Section | Why it's here | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting for approval** | Made with AI (Create photos and videos, or Claude). Drafts aren't used or shared until approved. On Enterprise, contributors' uploads land here too. | **Approve**, **Reject** (deletes it), **Open**. **Approve all** when there are several. |
| **Possible duplicates** | Looks like a copy of a file you already have. | **Delete this one** or **Keep both**. |
| **Off-brand** | Doesn't match your brand kit's imagery rules. | **Delete**, **Open**, or **It’s fine**. |
| **Couldn’t organise** | Mise couldn't read the file, so it has no tags or alt text yet. | **Open** or **Try again**. |
| **Products without a photo** | Products whose photo is missing. | **See products**, then drop {ID}.jpg files onto Assets. |
| **Brand kit** | There's no brand kit yet, or it's still a draft. | **Open brand kit**. |

## Who can decide
Owners, admins and editors approve and reject. On Enterprise, contributors and viewers can see the list and open items, but an editor, admin or owner decides.

## From Claude
In Claude, ask "What needs me in Mise?". It sees the same list and can approve, fix or tidy things when you say so.
