# Editing images and designs

Open a file and click **Edit**. How it's edited depends on where it came from. Editing is on **Pro**; on Free, Edit shows the upgrade pop-up.

Every edit is saved as a new version or a copy. The original is never lost. See [Versions](versions).

## Photos, logos and product photos
Crop, resize, rotate and adjust.

- **Crop:** drag on the picture for a free crop, drag the box to move it, or drag a corner to resize it.
- **Crop and size** presets:
  - **Crop:** Free, Original ratio, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9, 3:2.
  - **Email:** Full width 1200×600, Two column 560×560, Mobile hero 640×800 (based on your brand kit's email width).
  - **Social:** LinkedIn banner 1128×191, LinkedIn post 1200×627, Instagram post 1080×1350, Story 1080×1920, Square ad 1080×1080.
  - **Retail:** Marketplace square 2000×2000, Shopify product 2048×2048, Product portrait 1600×2000.
  - **Your team:** sizes your team saved. Click **+ Add a size for your team**, give it a name and a size, and everyone can use it (up to 30).
- **Rotate** left or right and **flip** horizontally or vertically.
- **Adjust:** Brightness, Contrast and Saturation (double-click a slider to reset), and **Remove white background** (saves as PNG). **Reset all** undoes everything.

If the crop is smaller than the size you chose, you'll be warned that it will be enlarged and may look soft.

**Save:**
- **Save as version {n}** replaces what everyone sees and keeps the old one in the history.
- **Save as copy** makes a new file named "{name} ({width}×{height})" and leaves the original alone.
- **Download** saves the result to your computer without saving it in Mise.

The file keeps its format (PNG and WebP stay as they are); others save as JPG.

**Words in a photo** are part of the picture and can't be edited here. Designs made in Create keep their words editable.

SVGs and videos can't be edited in Mise.

## Designs made in Create
Designs are changed where they're made: on a Create board. Open the design and click **Edit design**. It opens on the board it was made on, or a new one, where you can:
- **Double-click it** to change any words right on the design.
- **Say what to change** in the chat: "darker", "bigger headline", "use the knitwear photo", "add 20% off". Each change counts as a design.
- **Every size** to adapt it to the other sizes.
- **Save changes** to save it as a new version (the old one is kept).

See [Create designs](create-designs#change-a-design).

The asset page is for the file itself: its name, About, tags, alt text, licence and versions, plus the quick fixes above for photos. Anything that turns a file into something new (new versions, scenes, sizes for social, designs) happens in **Create**.

## Made in Figma
Designs made in Figma are edited in Figma, where their layers are. Say what to change and click **Make the change with Claude**: Claude edits the Figma frame and saves it back to Mise as a new version. **Open it in Figma** to edit it yourself.

## Products
**Edit** opens the product card: Label, Name, Description, Price, Button and Link, with a live preview. **Crop and adjust the photo** opens the photo tools. See [Products](products).

## Problems
| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This image couldn’t be loaded for editing." | Reload the page and try again. Very large files may need a smaller copy. |
| "The edited file is over the size limit." | Choose a smaller size preset. |
| "Couldn’t upload the edit." / "Couldn’t save the edit: …" | Check your connection and save again. |
| "Editing files is on Pro…" | Upgrade the brand to Pro. |
| "Contributors can change only the files they added." | On Enterprise, ask an editor to make the change, or ask an admin for editor access. |
