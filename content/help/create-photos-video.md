# Create photos and video

Mise makes new **photos** (a product in a new scene, a seasonal backdrop, a lifestyle shot) and short **video clips** with sound, in your brand's style. Photos and video are on **Pro**.

Mise picks the best AI model for each job and shows which one it used on every result.

## Make a photo
1. Open **Create** and choose **Photo** at the top.
2. **Describe the shot**: "Our trainers on a wet London street at dusk, soft reflections".
3. **Size** (optional): pick one, or leave it on **Auto**.
4. **Start from a photo** (optional): pick a product or photo. Mise keeps the product **exactly as it is** (shape, colours, logo) and only changes its surroundings.
5. Choose **1 take**, **2 takes** or **4 takes**.
6. Click **Make photo**.

## Make a video
1. Choose **Video** at the top.
2. **Describe the clip**: subject, movement, setting, mood and sound. "Slow push-in on the product on a marble counter, morning light, gentle café sounds."
3. **Shape**: **Reel or story** (9:16) or **Landscape banner** (16:9).
4. **Bring a photo to life** (optional): pick a product or photo to animate.
5. Length: 4, 6 or 8 seconds.
6. Click **Make video**.

A clip takes one to three minutes. You can carry on working; it saves itself.

## Where results go
Results appear under the composer, each tagged **Made with {model}**. Photos and videos are saved to **Review** as **drafts**: approve them to use or share them. **Make another** tries again with the same description.

## Which model Mise uses
| Job | Model | Counts as |
|---|---|---|
| A product in a new scene (from a photo) | Nano Banana 2 | 1 design per photo |
| Exact words on the image, or a hero shot | Nano Banana Pro | 2 designs per photo |
| Quick variations to choose from (no starting photo) | Imagen 4 Fast | 1 design per photo |
| Video clip | Veo 3.1 Fast | 10 designs per clip |

Mise chooses automatically. To choose yourself, click **Change model** under the composer. Words in quotation marks in your description ("SALE 20% OFF") make Mise use the model that spells words exactly.

## Tips for good results
- Describe the **setting, light and mood**, not just the object.
- For products, **start from the product photo** so the product stays exact.
- Your brand kit's **imagery style and do's and don'ts** are added to every request, so the better they are, the more on-brand the results.
- Ask for **2 or 4 takes** to choose from.

## Problems
| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No image came back… Try rewording the request." | The model declined or failed. Reword, especially anything that could read as unsafe, or avoid real people's names. |
| "The clip was blocked by the model’s safety filter." | Reword the description. |
| "The image took too long. Try again." | Try again. |
| "Making new images is on Pro…" / "Making video is on Pro…" | Upgrade the brand to Pro. |
| Photo and Video say they aren't switched on | They're temporarily unavailable. Try again later. |
| "That image can’t be animated…" | Pick a photo or image (not an SVG or a video) that's available. |
