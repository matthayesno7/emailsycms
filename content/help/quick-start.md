# Set up Mise in 10 minutes

## 1. Sign in
Go to misedam.com and click **Get your brand in order**. Continue with Google, or type your work email and click **Email me a link**, then open the link on the same device. There's no password and no card needed. See [Signing in](signing-in).

## 2. Build your brand kit from your website
1. Open **Brand kit** in the sidebar.
2. Type your website (for example `yourbrand.com`) and click **Build my brand kit**.
3. In about 20 seconds Mise reads your site's colours, fonts, buttons and logo, and drafts your kit. If your brand is still called "My brand", it's renamed after your site.
4. Check what it found. Fix anything that's off (a colour, a font), then click **Approve**.

Tip: if you started from the box on the Mise website, this happens automatically after you sign in.

More: [Your brand kit](brand-kit).

## 3. Bring in your files
On **Assets**, drop a folder of photos and logos anywhere on the page, or click **Add** and choose:
- **Upload files** or **Upload a folder** (folders keep their name, like Dropbox).
- **Add products** to connect Shopify, paste a product feed link, or upload a CSV.
- **Import from Drive, Dropbox or Box**.

Mise organises each file a few seconds after it lands. A strip at the top of Assets shows progress. More: [Adding files](adding-files), [Products](products).

## 4. Make your first design
1. Open **Create**.
2. Describe what you need, for example "Autumn sale email hero", or click one of the **Try** suggestions.
3. Pick a size, or leave it on **Auto**.
4. Click **Design 3 options**. Three designs appear in seconds, using your kit and photos.
5. Double-click any text to change it, then **Save** the one you like.

More: [Create designs](create-designs).

## 5. Share with your team and partners
- **Invite your team:** Settings → **Team** → type an email → **Invite**. People are free; Mise is priced per brand.
- **Share files:** open a file (or select several) → **Share** → **Create link**.
- **Brand portal:** **Sharing** → **Create brand portal**. One page for agencies and retailers with your approved files and brand guidelines, styled from your kit.

Sharing and publishing a portal are on Pro. See [Plans](plans).

## 6. Optional: connect Claude
Settings → **Claude** → **Create my link**, then add it to Claude. From then on you can ask Claude things like "Make a LinkedIn post for our new arrivals from our Mise brand kit" and it saves the result to Mise. See [Connect Claude](claude-connector).
