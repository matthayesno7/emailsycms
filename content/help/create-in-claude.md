# Making things in Claude and Figma

With the Mise connector on, Claude can make things for your brand too, and take on bigger jobs: a full email campaign, a set of social posts, a deck cover, or building designs in your Figma file.

## Make it in Claude
In **Create**, describe what you want and click **Make it in Claude** (or **Copy** to paste it yourself). Claude opens with a prompt that tells it to use your Mise brand kit and assets and save the result back to Mise.

The prompt library on Create has 29 ready-made prompts for email, social, ads, product shots, video and brand work. They're also in Claude: in Claude Code, type `/` and look under Mise.

## What Claude does
1. Reads your brand kit and finds the right photos, products and logos in Mise.
2. Makes the thing:
   - **Photos, video and finished images:** with Mise's own models.
   - **Editable designs (live text):** in your Figma file, if you connect Figma.
   Without Figma, Claude makes finished images. For designs you can edit afterwards in Mise, use **Create**.
3. Saves the result to Mise as a **draft** for your team to approve in **Review**.

## Do I need Figma?
No. Mise makes designs, photos and video itself. If your team designs in Figma, connect the Figma connector too and Claude can build in your files. Set your default Figma file in Settings → **Brand workspace** → **Connected Figma file**, so you don't have to paste the link every time. See [Using Mise with Figma](figma).
