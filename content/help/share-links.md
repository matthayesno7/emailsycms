# Share links

A share link is a page with the files you chose, styled with your brand kit. People don't need a Mise account to open it. Share links are on **Pro**. Editors, admins and owners can make them.

## Make a link
Start from:
- **One file**: open it → **Send to** → **Share link** (or click the picture → **Copy a share link**).
- **Several files**: tick them on Assets → **Share**.
- **A folder**: open it → **Share**. New files added to the folder later show up too.
- **A smart collection**: open it → **Share**. It keeps filling itself.

Then choose:
- **Title** and an optional **Message**.
- **Expires**: Never, 7 days, **30 days** (the default), or **Pick a date**.
- **Require a passcode** (at least 4 characters). Send the passcode separately from the link.
- **Allow downloads** and which formats: **Original** (full size, as uploaded), **Web** (up to 2000px) and **Email-ready** (1200px, compressed). Turn downloads off for view-only.

Click **Create link**. The link is copied for you ("Link copied"). **Open it** shows what people will see.

## What's included
- Up to 500 hand-picked files per link (if you pick more, the first 500 are shared). Drafts you picked by hand are included.
- Folder and collection links show up to 2,000 files.
- Folder and collection links never include drafts.
- Email blocks and files that are no longer available (archived, expired, obsolete) are never shared.

## See views, turn off, delete
**Sharing → Links** lists every live link with views and downloads, who it shares, and when it expires.
- **Copy** or **Open** a link.
- **Turn off** stops it straight away ("Link turned off. It stops working straight away."). **Turn on** brings it back.
- **Delete** (for turned-off or expired links) removes it.
- **Show expired and turned off** shows old links.

Views and downloads by your own team aren't counted.

## Problems
| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Use a passcode of at least 4 characters." | Make the passcode longer. |
| "Sharing is on Pro…" (the upgrade pop-up) | Upgrade the brand to share. |
| "That needs an editor, admin or owner of this brand." | Ask someone with that role to share it, or ask an admin for access. |
| "Couldn’t create the link. Try again." | Check your connection and try again. |
| Someone says the link doesn't work | Check **Sharing → Links**: it may have expired or been turned off. Send a new link. |
