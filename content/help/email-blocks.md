# Email blocks

An email block is an email module: images and copy together, ready to build in your email tool or for Claude to turn into a Figma component.

## Make a block
- **Add → New email block** and pick a type, or
- Open an image or product → **In email** → **Use in a block**, or
- On the **Email blocks** tab, drop images onto the page: each becomes a Hero (if wide) or a Card.

## Block types
| Type | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Hero** | Big image (600×400), headline (60), subhead (90), paragraph (300), button label (25) and link. |
| **Card** | Image on top, left or right; sub header (30), title (50), optional star rating, text (220), name (40), button (24) and link. |
| **Product** | One product: image, label (30), name (60), description (160), price (16), button (20) and link. **Fill from product** picks one from your feed. |
| **Button** | One call-to-action button: label (25) and link. |
| **Footer** | Logo, legal text (300), postal address (120) and unsubscribe line (40). |

Numbers are the most characters each field takes. The counter turns red if you go over.

## The block editor
- The preview shows the block in your brand's colours and fonts. Switch **Desktop** / **Mobile**, and **Show in an email** to see it in context.
- **Click any part** of the preview to jump to its field. Click an image to choose another from your library.
- **Alt text** for each image (up to 120 characters; 150 for products).
- **Save block** (or ⌘S). Mise makes each image email-ready at double resolution. "Block saved. Images are email-ready."
- If you leave with unsaved changes, you'll be asked to **Save**, **Discard** or **Stay**.

## Turn it into a Figma component
Once saved, **Make it a Figma component** shows a request for Claude. Click **Copy request for Claude**, paste it into Claude (with the Mise and Figma connectors on), and Claude builds it in your Figma file. The block then shows "In Figma".

## Delete a block
**Delete block** at the bottom of the editor. The images it used stay in your library.
