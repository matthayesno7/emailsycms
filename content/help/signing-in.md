# Signing in

Mise has no passwords. You sign in with Google or with a one-time link sent to your email. The same screen creates your account the first time.

## Sign in or create an account
1. Go to misedam.com and click **Get your brand in order**, or go straight to the app.
2. Choose one:
   - **Continue with Google**, then pick your Google account.
   - Type your **Work email** and click **Email me a link**. Open the email ("Check your inbox") and click the link **on the same device and browser**.
3. The first time, Mise creates your account and a brand called **My brand** (rename it any time, or build your brand kit from your website and it's renamed for you).

## If you were invited
Open the invite email and click the link, or just sign in with the same email address. Any brands you were invited to appear straight away.

## Common problems

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "That sign-in link has expired or was already used. Links work once, for an hour. Send yourself a new one." | Each link works once, for one hour. Email apps that "preview" links can use them up. | Send a new link and open it right away, or use Google. |
| "That sign-in link didn’t work. Send yourself a new one, or continue with Google." | The link was opened in a different browser, or was cut short. | Send a new link and open it in the same browser you requested it from. |
| "Email rate limit exceeded" (or similar) | Too many links were asked for in a short time. Wait a few minutes, or use Google. |
| No email arrives | It may be in spam or promotions, or your company filters outside mail. | Check spam, search for "Mise", wait a minute, or use Google. |
| You signed in but don't see a brand you were invited to | You signed in with a different email from the one invited. | Sign out (bottom of the sidebar) and sign in with the invited address, or ask for an invite to this one. |

## Signing out
Click **Sign out** under your email at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Changing your email
Your account is your email address. To use a different one, sign in with the new address and ask an owner or admin of each brand to invite it.
