# Team, invites and roles

Mise is priced per brand, not per person, so invite everyone who needs access. People are free on every plan.

## Invite someone
Settings → **Team** → type their email → **Invite**. On Enterprise, pick their role first.
- People without a Mise account get an invite email.
- People who already use Mise see the brand next time they open it ("{email} already has an account…").
- Pending invites show as **Invited**, with **Cancel**.

## Roles on Free and Pro
| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing, inviting and removing people, and deleting the brand. |
| **Editor** | Everything else: add, organise, edit, approve, share, make designs and edit the brand kit. |

Everyone you invite joins as an **Editor**. Owners can make an editor an owner.

## Roles on Enterprise
Enterprise brands have five roles, so you can let people in without letting them change everything.

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and deleting the brand. |
| **Admin** | Everything except billing and deleting the brand. Invites people, changes roles, changes brand settings, sees the [Activity log](activity-log), approves the brand kit. |
| **Editor** | Adds, edits, approves, shares and deletes; edits the brand kit; makes things. |
| **Contributor** | Adds files and makes things, which land in **Review** as drafts for an editor to approve. Changes and deletes only their own files. Can't approve, share or change the brand kit. |
| **Viewer** | Browses, searches and downloads. Can't add, change or share anything. |

These rules apply everywhere: in the app, in share and portal settings, and through Claude.

## Change someone's role
Settings → **Team** → pick a new role next to their name.
- Admins can change anyone below owner.
- Only owners can make someone an owner or change an owner.
- A brand always keeps at least one owner. To step down, make someone else an owner first.

## Remove someone
Settings → **Team** → **Remove** next to their name. They lose access to this brand straight away. Their files stay.

## Problems
| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Only admins and owners can invite people…" | Ask an admin or owner to invite them. |
| "Admin, contributor and viewer roles are on Enterprise. Invite them as an editor." | Your brand isn't on Enterprise. |
| "Only an owner can make or change an owner." | Ask an owner. |
| "A brand needs at least one owner. Make someone else an owner first." | Promote someone else before changing your own role. |
| "Enter a valid email address." | Check the email for typos. |
