# What Mise is and how it works

Mise is a brand asset library built for the AI era. It's one place for your brand's logos, photos, product shots, videos and designs, with AI doing the admin and your team making the decisions.

**AI does the work.** Every file is described, tagged and checked against your brand kit a few seconds after it arrives, so you can find anything by describing it. Mise makes new designs, product photos and short videos from your own assets, in your brand's style.

**People approve it.** Photos and videos AI makes from scratch land as drafts in **Review**. Drafts aren't used in designs, portals or folder links until someone on your team approves them.

**It works where you work.** Connect Mise to Claude (and other AI tools) and they use your real brand kit and files instead of guessing, then save what they make back to Mise.

## The main parts of the app

The sidebar has five pages:

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Review** | Things that need a person: drafts to approve, possible duplicates, off-brand files, files Mise couldn't read. The number shows how many are waiting. |
| **Assets** | Your library. Upload, import, search, organise into folders and smart collections, and open any file to see its details, download it, edit it or share it. |
| **Create** | Describe what you need. Mise designs it (three options), or makes a new photo or video clip, from your brand kit and your own photos. |
| **Sharing** | Share links and brand portals for agencies, retailers, press and partners, with views and downloads. |
| **Brand kit** | Your colours, fonts, logos, button style, imagery rules and tone of voice. Everything Mise makes and checks follows it. |

At the bottom of the sidebar are **Help** (ask a question or browse these articles), **Feedback** and **Settings** (brand settings, plan and usage, team and the Claude connector).

## Brands

Everything in Mise belongs to a **brand** (sometimes called a workspace): its own library, brand kit, portals, team and plan. If you run several brands, switch between them from the brand name at the top of the sidebar. See [Brands](brands).

## Who it's for

- Teams keeping brand files in Google Drive or Dropbox who are tired of hunting for the right logo.
- Teams on a traditional DAM (digital asset management system) who want AI without a six-figure contract or a long rollout.
- Anyone using Claude or another AI tool who wants it to work from their real brand, not a guess.
