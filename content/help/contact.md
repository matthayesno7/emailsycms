# Still stuck?

1. **Ask Mise.** Click **Help** at the bottom of the sidebar and ask your question in your own words. It answers from this help centre and links you to the right article.
2. **Search this help centre**, or check [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting) for the exact message you see.
3. **Send feedback.** Click **Feedback** at the bottom of the sidebar, choose **Something’s broken**, **A question**, **An idea** or **Something I like**, and describe what you did and what happened. Mise attaches the page and brand you were on, so you don't need to explain where. It goes straight to the team.
4. **Enterprise and sales:** [book a call](https://calendar.notion.so/meet/matthayes/3363f4yal).
