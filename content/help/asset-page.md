# The asset page

Click any file on **Assets** to open its page. Use **←** and **→** to move between files, and **Esc** or **← Library** to go back.

## At the top
- **The name**: click to rename it.
- **Where it came from** (Uploaded, From the feed, Made in Create, Generated, Made in Figma), its size, file size, format and version.
- **Edit**: quick fixes to this file (crop, resize, rotate, adjust). For designs made in Create, **Edit design** opens it on a Create board. See [Editing images and designs](editing).
- **Open in Create**: puts the picture on a board where you can resize it for social, give it a new scene, light or season, clean the background, translate the words, mock it up, animate it or describe any other edit. See [Make and edit pictures you already have](create-photos-video#make-and-edit-pictures-you-already-have).
- **Download**:
  - **Original file**, exactly as uploaded.
  - **Web, 2000px** and **Email-ready, 1200px**, each as JPG, PNG or WebP. These only appear when the original is bigger than that.
- **Copy image**: copies it, ready to paste into Figma (⌘V), or select a layer in Figma and press ⌘⇧R to replace its image.

Click the picture itself for a quick menu: Edit, Copy image, Download, Copy a share link, Set focal point and zoom.

## Focal point
**Focal point** (photos only): click the part of the picture that must always stay in view. Every crop, email block and design Mise makes from this photo keeps that spot in frame.

## The side panel

**About**: the description, tags, colours, brand check, text found in the image, and the product it shows. Everything here is filled in by AI and you can change it; your changes are kept. See [How Mise organises your files](auto-organise).
- **Colours**: click a swatch to copy its hex code.
- **Product link**: "Is this {product}? **Yes, link it** · No", or "Shows {product} · **Not this product**".

**Availability**: archive it, set a licence end date, or mark it obsolete. See [No longer available](availability).

**Versions**: earlier versions and **Restore**. See [Versions and undo](versions).

**Send to**:
- **Email**: downloads the email-ready copy for Klaviyo, Mailchimp and other email tools.
- **Figma**: Claude places it in your Figma file at full size (or drag the image in yourself).
- **Share link**: a link with expiry, passcode and download counts. See [Share links](share-links).

**Folder** and **Type** (Image, Logo, Product): change them here.

**Alt text**: what the image shows, for people who can't see it. Up to 300 characters, with a copy button.

**In email**: **Use in a block** makes an email block from this file (a logo becomes a Footer, a wide image a Hero, anything else a Card), and lists the blocks that already use it.

**Delete asset**: deletes it for everyone, with every version. You'll be asked to confirm.

## Generated files
Files made by AI show **Generated · waiting for approval** with an **Approve** button, the prompt used, and the model that made it. Until approved, they aren't used in designs, portals or folder and collection links.

## Products
Products open on their **Product card**. See [Products](products#the-product-page).

## Videos
Videos play on the page and download in their original format. Most email inboxes don't play video, so for email use a still or an animated GIF with a link to the video.
