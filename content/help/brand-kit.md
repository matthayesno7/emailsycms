# Your brand kit

Your brand kit is your brand's colours, fonts, logos, button style, email layout, imagery rules and tone of voice. Everything Mise makes follows it, and every photo is checked against it. Claude uses it too. Each brand has one kit.

## Build it
Open **Brand kit**. Choose one:

### From your website (fastest)
Type your website (for example `yourbrand.com`) and click **Build my brand kit**. In about 20 seconds Mise reads your colours, fonts, buttons and logo, and your share image as a reference photo. Your logo and that photo are added to your library.

Sites that build their pages with a lot of JavaScript give thinner results. If something's missing, add it by hand or try your main shop page instead.

### From your Figma design system
If your team keeps its foundations in Figma, turn on the Mise and Figma connectors in Claude, then click **Copy request for Claude** and paste it into Claude. Claude reads your Figma file and saves the kit here as a draft. See [Connect Claude](claude-connector).

### Blank
**Or start with a blank kit** and fill it in yourself.

## What's in it
- **Logos**: Main logo, On dark, and Icon, chosen from your Logos. If there are none, upload your logo first.
- **Colours**: Primary, Secondary, Accent, Text, Muted text, Background, Surface, Border, Link, Button and Button text.
- **Type**: Headings and Body: font family, weight, a **web font link** (a Google Fonts CSS link), and a fallback font. Sizes for H1, H2, Body and Small, and **Headline case** (As typed, UPPERCASE, Title Case).
- **Buttons and layout**: button style (Filled, Outline, Underlined link), case, corner radius, padding and weight; email width (480–800, usually 600), corners and spacing.
- **Imagery**: describe your photo style, list **Do** and **Don’t** rules (up to 8 each), and pick up to 6 **Reference images**. These drive the brand check on every photo, and the style of new photos Mise makes.
- **Voice**: up to 6 tone words and **Notes for Claude** (up to 1,000 characters).

The preview on the right shows an email in your brand as you edit.

## Checks
At the top, Mise lists anything **Still missing**, plus warnings:
- **Contrast:** text, button text or links that are hard to read on their background (below the accessibility guidelines of 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for links).
- **Fonts:** a font with no web font link. Most inboxes then show your fallback font, so choose a fallback that looks close.

## Approve it
Edits are saved as a draft ("Unsaved changes", then **Save**). **Approve** (or **Save and approve**) makes it the kit everyone uses. Any later edit puts it back to draft until it's approved again.

Owners and admins approve the kit. Editors can edit it. Everyone else sees "An admin or owner approves the kit."

A draft kit still works, but Mise and Claude mention that it isn't approved.

## Refresh from your website
**Refresh from website** updates what the site shows and keeps your logos, reference images and notes.

## Team image sizes
Sizes your team adds in the photo editor (**+ Add a size for your team**) are saved with the brand kit, so everyone sees them.
