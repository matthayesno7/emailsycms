# Folders and smart collections

## Folders
Folders work like folders in Dropbox: a file is in one folder, or none.

- **Make one:** **Add → New folder**, or **Folder** in the row above the grid. Names can be up to 60 characters.
- **Upload a folder** from your computer and it arrives as a Mise folder with the same name.
- **Move files in:** drag a tile onto a folder in the row, select several and use **Move to…**, or change **Folder** on the file's page.
- **Open a folder** to see its files, then use **Share** (everyone with the link sees new files added later too), **Rename** or **Delete folder**.
- **Deleting a folder** never deletes the files: "Folder deleted. Its files are still in All files."

## Smart collections
A smart collection is a saved search that fills itself: new files that match land in it automatically. A file can be in many collections.

### Make one
- Click **Smart collection** in the row above the grid, or
- Search for something and click **Save search**, or
- When your library has a few tagged files, click one of the **Suggested collections**.

### Rules
- **Name**, for example "Lifestyle, summer".
- **Tags**: comma-separated. With more than one, choose **Any** or **All**.
- **Describe it**: matched by meaning, for example "lifestyle shots for email".
- **Types**: Images, Logos, Products, Videos.
- **Brand check**: Any, On brand or Off brand.

The window shows how many files match right now. Click **Save**.

### Edit, share or delete
Open the collection, then **Edit** or **Share** in the line under its name. **Delete collection** is in the edit window. Deleting a collection never deletes files.

## Who can do what
Editors, admins and owners can create, rename and delete folders and collections. On Enterprise, contributors can create folders (for example by uploading one) but not rename or delete them.
