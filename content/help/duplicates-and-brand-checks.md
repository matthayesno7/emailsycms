# Duplicates and brand checks

## Possible duplicates
When a file looks like a copy of one you already have (even resized or re-saved), Mise flags it rather than deciding for you:
- A **Duplicate?** badge on its tile.
- "Looks like a copy of “{name}”" on its page, with **Open it**, **Keep both** and **Delete this copy**.
- An entry under **Possible duplicates** in [Review](review).

**Keep both** clears the flag for good. To see every flagged file, choose **Possible duplicates** in the filter on Assets.

Products are never flagged as copies of other products, because different products photographed the same way look alike.

## Brand checks
If your brand kit has imagery rules (a style, plus do's and don'ts), every photo is checked against them.
- **On brand** or **Off brand**, with the reason, on the file's page.
- An **Off-brand** badge on its tile, and an entry under **Off-brand** in Review.

If it's fine, click **It’s fine** in Review (it's marked "Checked by a person" and won't be flagged again). If it isn't, delete it or archive it.

Better rules mean better checks: see [Your brand kit](brand-kit#whats-in-it).
