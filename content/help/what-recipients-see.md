# What the people you share with see

People you share with don't need a Mise account (except for invite-only portals, where they confirm their email).

## A share link
A page with your logo, colours and fonts, the title and message, and the files. They can search (when there are more than six files), open any file to view it, and download it in the formats you allowed. A file opens full screen, with **← Previous** and **Next →**.

- **Downloads off:** "Viewing only: downloads are turned off for this link."
- **Passcode:** "Enter the passcode". After the right passcode they stay unlocked for 30 days on that device.
- **Expired or turned off:** "This link has expired" / "This link has been turned off", with "Ask the person who shared it for a new one."
- **Files no longer available** are left out, with a note saying how many and to ask you for replacements.

## A brand portal
Your files grouped into sections (logos first, then your folders and collections), a search box, and a **Brand guidelines** page: logos to download, colours to copy (hex and RGB), type, buttons, imagery do's and don'ts, and tone of voice.

- **Invite-only portals:** "Sign in to {brand}’s portal". They type their work email and get a sign-in link. If their email isn't on the list: "You don’t have access… Ask {brand} to add you."
- **Unpublished:** "This portal isn’t published yet."
- **Wrong address:** "This portal isn’t available. Check the address, or ask the brand for a new link."
- **Too many sign-in emails:** "Too many sign-in emails just now. Try again in a little while."

## If someone is stuck
| They see | Tell them |
|---|---|
| "That passcode isn’t right." | Check the passcode you sent them. If you changed it, send the new one. |
| "Too many tries. Wait a minute and try again." | Wait a minute and type it again. |
| "This link isn’t available" | The link was mistyped, turned off or deleted. Send a new one. |
| No sign-in email for an invite-only portal | Check spam. Make sure their email (or their company's `@domain`) is on the invite list. |
| A file has no download button | Downloads are off for that link or portal, or that size isn't available for that file. |

Every page says "Shared with Mise" at the bottom and is hidden from search engines.
