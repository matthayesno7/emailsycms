# Privacy, security and AI

## Where your files live
Your files and data are stored in the EU. Mise is run by HW Tech Ltd, trading as Mise, in London. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Terms](/terms).

## How AI is used
Mise sends the content each feature needs to its AI providers: Claude (Anthropic) to organise files, search and design; Google's models for new photos and video; and a search provider to make files searchable by meaning. They work under terms that **don't let them train on your content**.

- Mise **never identifies people from their faces**.
- AI suggestions never overwrite your team's edits.
- Photos and video AI makes from scratch are **drafts** until a person approves them.

## Who can see your brand
- **Your team**: only people invited to the brand. On Enterprise, roles control what each person can do. See [Team and roles](team-and-roles).
- **People you share with**: only the files in the links and portals you create, in the formats you allow. Add a passcode, expiry date or invite list for more control. See [Share links](share-links) and [Brand portals](brand-portals).
- **Claude**: only through your own connector link, with your role and only in your brands.

Share and portal pages are hidden from search engines. Photos are shown as their web or email-ready copy where there is one. Download links expire after a couple of minutes. For logos and videos that must not be downloaded, share them only with people you trust: SVG logos and videos are shown as the original file.

## Keeping access tidy
- Remove people who leave: Settings → Team → **Remove**.
- Turn off share links you no longer need: Sharing → Links → **Turn off**.
- Treat your Claude connector link like a password. If it's shared by mistake, Settings → Claude → **Turn off**, then create a new one.
- On Enterprise, check the [Activity log](activity-log) for who did what.

## Payments
Payments are handled by Stripe. Mise never sees or stores your card number.

## Questions about data
For data requests (a copy of your data, or deleting your account), send a message through **Feedback** or see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
