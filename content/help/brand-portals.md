# Brand portals

A brand portal is a public page with your approved logos, images and product shots, plus brand guidelines made from your brand kit, styled with your logo, colours and fonts. Share one link with agencies, retailers, press and partners instead of sending files around.

## Create a portal
**Sharing → Brand portals → Create brand portal**. Mise creates "{brand} brand portal" with everything approved, styled from your kit, and opens it for editing.

On **Free** you can build and **preview** a portal. **Publishing it** so others can open it is on **Pro**.

## Set it up
- **Name** and **Address**: the portal lives at `misedam.com/p/{brand}/{portal}`.
- **Intro**: an optional line at the top, for example "Approved images for press. Please credit Oakhaus."
- **What’s in it**:
  - **Everything approved** (grouped by folder), or only the **Folders** and **Smart collections** you choose.
  - **Brand guidelines page**: logos, colours, fonts, and imagery do's and don'ts, from your brand kit.
  - Drafts are never shown.
- **Who can open it**:
  - **Anyone with the link**.
  - **Passcode** (at least 4 characters). Changing it locks out everyone who used the old one.
  - **Invite list**: emails, or `@company.com` for a whole company, one per line (up to 500). Anything that isn't an email or `@domain` is ignored, so check the list after saving. Visitors sign in with a link sent to their email, and you see who downloaded what.
- **Downloads**: allow them, and choose Original, Web (2000px) and Email-ready.
- **Status**: **Published**. Turn it off to hide the portal without deleting it.

Click **Save**. **Open portal** and **Copy link** are at the top.

## Several portals
Make one per audience, for example Press, Retail partners and Agencies: **New portal**. `misedam.com/p/{brand}` then shows a "Choose a portal" page.

## Your brand address
Every portal address starts with your brand address, `/p/{brand}`. Owners and admins can change it under **Brand address → Change**. Old portal links stop working when you do.

## Stats
Each portal shows visits and downloads for the last 30 days, the top downloads and, for invite-list portals, who downloaded. Your own team's visits aren't counted.

## Delete a portal
**Delete portal** at the bottom of the editor. The files stay in your library.
