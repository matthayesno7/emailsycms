# Importing from Google Drive, Dropbox and Box

**Assets → Add → Import from Drive, Dropbox or Box** opens "Import from where your images live".

Files are **copied** into Mise, so the originals stay where they are. If a folder is open in Mise, the files land in that folder.

## Google Drive
1. Click **Google Drive** and sign in to Google if asked.
2. Pick the images and videos you want. You can pick several at once, including from shared drives.
3. Click **Select**. Mise only gets access to the files you picked, never your whole Drive.

## Dropbox
1. Click **Dropbox** and sign in if asked.
2. Pick images and videos (several at once is fine) and click **Choose**.

## Box
1. Click **Box**. The first time, sign in to Box and allow Mise. You'll then see "Connected as {account}".
2. Browse your folders. Then either:
   - Click **Import folder** next to a folder to bring in everything inside it (subfolders too) into a Mise folder with the same name, or
   - Tick files and click **Import {n} selected**.
3. **Disconnect** removes Mise's access to Box.

## While it imports
Progress shows as "Copying from {source}… 12 of 40". When it's done you'll see something like "38 added from Dropbox, 2 already here".

- **Already imported?** Files you brought in before are skipped, so it's safe to import the same folder again.
- **Formats:** PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, SVG, AVIF, MP4, WebM and MOV.
- **Sizes:** up to 100 MB per file, and images up to 50 MB.

## Problems
| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| A source shows **Soon** or "Not switched on yet" | That source is temporarily unavailable. Download the files and use **Upload a folder** instead. |
| "Box needs connecting again." | Click Box and sign in again. |
| "No images or videos in that folder." | The folder only has other file types (PDFs, documents). |
| "Box didn’t respond." / "Dropbox didn’t load…" / "Google Drive didn’t load…" | Make sure pop-ups aren't blocked for Mise and try again in a few minutes. If it keeps happening, download the files and use **Upload a folder**. |
| "{n} couldn’t be copied" | Usually a file over the size limit or in an unsupported format. Upload it directly instead. |
