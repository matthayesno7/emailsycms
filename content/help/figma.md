# Using Mise with Figma

You don't need Figma to use Mise. If your team designs in Figma, Mise fits in.

## Get files into Figma yourself
- **Drag** any image from Mise onto the Figma canvas. It drags the email-ready copy.
- **Copy and paste:** **Copy image** on a file's page (or the copy button on a tile), then ⌘V in Figma.
- **Replace an image:** copy it in Mise, select the layer in Figma and press ⌘⇧R.

## Connect your Figma file
Settings → **Brand workspace** → **Connected Figma file**: paste the link to your brand's design-system or email file (in Figma: Share → Copy link). Claude uses it whenever you don't give it a link, and it's where images, blocks and components from this brand go.

## With Claude
Turn on both the Mise and Figma connectors in Claude (see [Connect Claude](claude-connector)). Then Claude can:
- **Build designs in Figma** from your brand kit and Mise assets, with live text, and save the finished frame to Mise.
- **Place Mise images** in Figma at full size.
- **Turn email blocks into Figma components**: open a block → **Make it a Figma component** → **Copy request for Claude**.
- **Edit a design made in Figma**: open it in Mise → **Edit in Figma** → say what to change. Claude changes the Figma frame and saves it back as a new version.
- **Build your brand kit** from your Figma design system.

## Figma's own AI models
Claude can also use Figma's image and video models. Those runs use your Figma credits. Claude tells you the cost and asks before it runs one. Mise's own photo and video tools (see [Create photos and video](create-photos-video)) don't use Figma credits.
