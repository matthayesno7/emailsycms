# Finding files

## Search
Press **/** anywhere to jump to the search box on **Assets**, or click it.

- **Words** match names, tags, descriptions, product IDs, colour names and text inside images. Every word you type has to match the start of a word, so "bea" finds "beach".
- **Meaning**: results are ranked by what files *show*, not just their words. "Woman outdoors with a blue bag" finds the right photo even if nobody tagged it that way.
- **Plain English**: type three or more words, such as "red product shots, landscape", and Mise turns them into filters. You'll see "Reading your search…", then chips like **Products**, **Red**, **Landscape**. Click a chip to remove that filter. Words it couldn't turn into a filter are still searched ("matching …").

Filters Mise understands include: images, logos, products and videos; colours; landscape, portrait or square; a folder ("in Summer 2026"); on-brand or off-brand; made with AI, from the feed or uploaded; drafts or approved.

## Save a search
While there's text in the search box, click **Save search** to make it a **smart collection**: a folder that fills itself as new matching files arrive. See [Folders and smart collections](folders-and-collections).

## Tabs
The row of tabs above the grid groups files by type and size:
**All**, **Photos**, **Email banners**, **LinkedIn banners**, **LinkedIn posts & ads**, **Social posts**, **Stories**, **Thumbnails & slides**, **Display ads**, **Videos**, **Logos**, **Products** and **Email blocks**.

Tabs only appear when they have something in them. Images are sorted into size tabs by their exact pixel size (for example 1080×1920 goes under Stories); anything else is under Photos.

## Where files came from
The dropdown next to the tabs filters by origin: **From anywhere**, **Uploaded**, **From feed**, **Made with Claude**, and, when there are any, **To review**, **Possible duplicates** and **No longer available**.

Files that are archived, expired or obsolete only appear under **No longer available**. See [No longer available](availability).

## On a tile
- **To review**: a draft waiting for approval.
- **Duplicate?**: looks like a copy of another file.
- **Off-brand**: doesn't match your brand kit's imagery rules (hover for the reason).
- **Licence ends {date}**: its licence runs out within 30 days.
- **Archived / Licence expired / Obsolete**: no longer available.
- Videos play when you hover over them.

## Selecting several files
Tick the box on a tile. Once anything is selected, clicking tiles adds them. The bar at the bottom lets you **Share**, **Make…** new versions (see [Make…](create-photos-video#start-from-a-picture-you-already-have-make)), **Move to…** a folder, **Select all** or **Clear**. Press Esc to clear.
