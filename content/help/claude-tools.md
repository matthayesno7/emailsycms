# What Claude can do with Mise

With the connector on, you can run Mise by asking Claude. It always reads your brand kit first, never uses files that are no longer available, and saves what it makes as **drafts** for your team to approve. It asks before deleting, approving or publishing anything.

## Example requests
- "What needs me in Mise?"
- "Find our best lifestyle photos with a blue bag."
- "Make an autumn sale email hero for {brand}."
- "Put our new trainers on a wet London street at dusk." (photo)
- "Make an 8-second reel of the Soleos watch on a marble counter." (video)
- "Crop the summer hero to an Instagram story."
- "Make a view-only link to the Press folder that lasts 7 days." (Claude makes the link; you send it.)
- "Mark the 2024 logo obsolete and point people to the new one."
- "Build our brand kit from our Figma design system."
- "Every Monday, tell me what's new in Mise and what needs approving." (as a scheduled task in Claude)

## What it can do
| Area | What Claude can do |
|---|---|
| **Find** | List your brands; search files by meaning; find products; open a file's details and image. |
| **Brand kit** | Read it; build or refresh it from your website or a Figma file (saved as a draft); approve it when you say so (admins and owners). |
| **Make** | Make photos and finished images (Mise picks the model, up to 4 takes) and short video clips; build editable designs in your Figma file (with Figma connected) and save them to Mise; save any finished image or video to Mise. |
| **Review** | See the Review list; approve, reject, keep both, mark on-brand, retry. |
| **Organise** | Rename, retag, describe, move to folders, create smart collections, set availability, licence dates and replacements. |
| **Edit** | Crop and resize photos to named sizes, rotate, flip, and adjust brightness and saturation; list versions and restore one. |
| **Share** | Create, change and turn off share links; create and publish portals. |
| **Bring in** | Import images and videos from links; give you an upload link for files on your computer. |
| **Set up** | Create a brand (extra brands go through checkout); invite a teammate (admins and owners; with a role on Enterprise). |

## It follows your plan and role
Claude can only do what you could do in the app. If you're a viewer it can look but not change; if the brand is on Free it can't share, edit or make photos and video; if you're a contributor what it makes waits for an editor.

## Costs
Photos, video and designs Claude makes with Mise count towards the brand's designs, just like in the app. See [Plans](plans).

If you also use Figma's own AI models through Claude, those use your Figma credits. Claude tells you the cost and asks first.
